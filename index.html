WHO WE ARE

Formulated, bottled and delivered — nothing outsourced.

Multi Manufacturers is a Harare-based formulator of bulk industrial catering and household cleaning chemicals, working out of 8 George Avenue, Msasa. We mix, test and bottle every product on site, from hospital-grade disinfectants to the bubble bath in a family bathroom.

That control over the whole process is what keeps prices affordable without cutting quality — and lets hotels, factories and households order the same reliable formula, batch after batch.
